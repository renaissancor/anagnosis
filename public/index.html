Which states succeeded which, and who decided that?

Anagnosis is a dataset of political entities and how they succeeded one another. It grows by curation, one judged claim at a time, so its coverage is uneven by design.

How to read it

Everything on this site is one of two things, and the two never share a style.

Ink is decided

A person judged this claim and cited a source for it. It looks like this, with a line down its side.

Pencil is computed. A script found an overlap in territory, language or religion, and nobody has judged it. Rivals and contemporaries appear here too, so read it as a lead, never as history.

A gap means not yet curated. It does not mean nothing happened.
