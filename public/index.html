Which states succeeded which, and who decided that?

Anagnosis is a dataset of political entities and how they succeeded one another. It grows by curation, one judged claim at a time, so its coverage is uneven by design.

How to read it

A statement about succession here is one of two things, and the two never share a style.

Ink is decided

A person judged this claim and cited a source for it. A claim has a green line down its side, an arrow from the source to the heir, and the cited source at its foot.

Pencil is computed. A script found an overlap in territory, language, ruling people or religion, and nobody has judged it. It is set in small grey sans type under the heading Computed evidence, or as dotted lines. Rivals and contemporaries appear too, so read it as a lead, never as history.

Other text, such as polity notes and the history tables, is curated description. Words like successor there are not decided claims. A gap means not yet curated; it does not mean nothing happened.
